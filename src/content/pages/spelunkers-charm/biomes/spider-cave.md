The Spider Cave is a new underground biome, added with [TerraBlender](https://modrinth.com/mod/terrablender). It's dark, quiet and crawling.

<figure><img src="/mods/spelunkers-charm/media/spider_cave_slide.webp" alt="The Spider Cave biome" /></figure>

## What's inside

- Walls and ceilings are coated in [Web Veins](../../items/web_vein/), [Packed Webs](../../items/packed_web/) and [Hanging Webs](../../items/hanging_web/).
- [Spider Eggs](../../items/spider_egg/) cling to the stone. They slowly crack when a player is nearby and hatch into **baby cave spiders**. Step on them carelessly and they break, so sneak to walk past safely.
- Its own eerie ambient sounds play now and then.
- Tremors never happen here.

## Where to find it

It generates **underground** below warm, humid, inland areas, the kind of land where jungles grow. It replaces part of the normal cave biome there.

## Mobs

Spiders, zombies, skeletons, creepers and slimes spawn here in the dark like in any cave, along with the occasional enderman, witch or zombie villager. Bats flutter around, and glow squid swim in its water.
