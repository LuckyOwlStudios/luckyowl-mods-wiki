## Brewing

Brew an **Awkward Potion** with a [Cave Mushroom](../../items/cave_mushroom/) to get a Potion of Haste.

| Potion | Brewed from | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Potion of Haste** | Awkward Potion + Cave Mushroom | Haste for 3:00 |
| **Potion of Haste** (long) | Potion of Haste + Redstone | Haste for 8:00 |
| **Potion of Haste II** | Potion of Haste + Glowstone Dust | Haste II for 1:30 |

Add **Gunpowder** for a splash potion, then **Dragon's Breath** for a lingering one, just like vanilla potions.
