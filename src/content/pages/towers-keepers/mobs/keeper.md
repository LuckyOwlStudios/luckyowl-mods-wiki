Keepers are the heart of the mod: small, boxy folk in red robes with glowing eyes. They work like villagers, so they pick up a job from a job site block and trade with you.

## Professions

A Keeper can take any villager job: Librarian, Mason, Cartographer, Fletcher, Toolsmith, Weaponsmith, Shepherd, Fisherman, Butcher, Armorer, Farmer, Cleric and Leatherworker. Each job gives it its own outfit.

<figure><img src="/mods/towers-keepers/media/professions.png" alt="Keepers in each of their professions" /></figure>

## Trading

Right-click a Keeper to trade. Discounts from your reputation and **Hero of the Village** work the same as with villagers. A Keeper with no trades, or a baby, shakes its head at you instead.

## Towers

A [Tower Keeper](../tower-keeper/) can call Keepers over and climb on top of them, stacking them into a living tower.
