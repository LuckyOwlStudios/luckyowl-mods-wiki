The Gildsmith is a tall Keeper, three blocks high, with gold-rimmed glasses, a long moustache and a tall green hat. What it does is still being made, and this page will grow when it's ready.

## Behavior

Like the other special Keepers, it sleeps at night, runs when hurt and follows you if you hold carrots, potatoes or beetroots.
