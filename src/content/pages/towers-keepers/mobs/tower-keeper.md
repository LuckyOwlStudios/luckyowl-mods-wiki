The Tower Keeper is the leader of the Keepers, and the reason for the mod's name.

## Building a tower

Every few seconds the Tower Keeper casts a call for help. Every free [Keeper](../keeper/) within 20 blocks walks over to it. When the closest one gets near, the Tower Keeper climbs on top. Then it calls again, and the next Keeper to arrive goes underneath, so it ends up riding a stack **two Keepers high**. The whole stack turns together, facing the same way as the Keeper at the bottom.

## Behavior

Like the other special Keepers, it sleeps at night, runs when hurt and follows you if you hold carrots, potatoes or beetroots.
