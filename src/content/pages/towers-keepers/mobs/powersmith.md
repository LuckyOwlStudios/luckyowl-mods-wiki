The Powersmith is a wide, chest-shaped Keeper with a heavy belt. It can **empower** your gear, raising one enchantment one level above its normal max.

## Empowering

Right-click the Powersmith to open the **Empower** screen. It looks like an enchanting table, with a slot for your item, a slot for amethyst and a list of the item's enchantments. Items with more than three enchantments scroll with the mouse wheel.

- Only enchantments already at their **max level** can be raised. The others are greyed out.
- Hover a row to see what it would become and what it costs.
- Each upgrade costs **16 Amethyst Shards**. Creative mode skips the cost.
- Each item can be empowered **once**, for one enchantment. An empowered item gets a bold blue **Empowered** line in its tooltip.
- **Enchanted books** work too, using the enchantments stored in them.
- Enchantments that only have one level, like Mending and Infinity, can't be empowered, since a second level would do nothing.

## Stats

| Stat | Value |

Like the other special Keepers, it sleeps at night, runs when hurt and follows you if you hold carrots, potatoes or beetroots.
