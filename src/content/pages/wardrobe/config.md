---
title: "Config"
summary: "Lucky's Wardrobe's setting for mobs wearing and dropping cosmetics."
---

Settings live in `config/luckyswardrobe-common.toml`, under `mobSpawning`.

| Option | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `mobCosmetics` | `0.05` | Chance (`0.0` to `1.0`) that a mob spawns wearing a cosmetic, and that it drops its cosmetic when killed (like a witch dropping her hat). `0` turns both off. |
