## How it works

Load **gunpowder** as the fuse and a **block** as the ammo, set the power from 1 to 4, then fire it with flint and steel, a redstone signal, or by sitting at it and pressing the Fire Cannon key. Each point of power burns one gunpowder.

- **Cannonballs** and **bombs** have their own effects.
- **TNT** explodes on impact.
- **Melons** and **dried kelp blocks** burst into their drops.
- **Any other block** flies, lands and places itself where it hits.

A full-power shot has a small chance to damage the cannon. Repair it with an **iron ingot**.
