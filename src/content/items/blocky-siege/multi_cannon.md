## How it works

Load **gunpowder** and **any item**, set the burst count from 1 to 4, and fire. Each round uses one gunpowder and one item.

- **Arrows**, **fire charges**, **snowballs** and other throwables fly as themselves.
- **Firework stars** burst in the air.
- **Anything else** flies out and drops where it lands.

Repair it with an **iron ingot**.
