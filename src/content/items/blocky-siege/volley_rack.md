## How it works

Load **gunpowder** and up to **8 stacks** of arrows, fireworks or other projectiles. While it has a redstone signal, or while you hold the fire key at it, it launches a shot every few ticks from a random stack. Each shot uses one gunpowder.

Repair it with **planks**.
