## How it works

Fill its one-bucket tank with **buckets**, **potions** or any modded fluid container, then fire. Hold the fire key while manning it to keep spraying.

- **Water** puts out fires and wets farmland.
- **Lava** sprays burning embers that set things alight.
- **Potions** splash their effect where they land.

Empty bottles and buckets placed in its slot are filled back up from the tank. Repair it with an **iron ingot**.
