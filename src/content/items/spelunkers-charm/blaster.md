## How it works

1. **Load it.** Open the Blaster and put a **lava**, **water** or **powder snow bucket** in its slot.
2. **Aim it.** It can face any direction, including up and down.
3. **Power it** with redstone. It fires a **5-block jet** out of its front.

## What the jet does

- **Every jet** pushes mobs and players away from the Blaster.
- **Lava** also sets them on fire.
- **Powder snow** also freezes them.
- **Water** only pushes, so it's safe to use on yourself.

Great for launchers, traps and mob funnels.
