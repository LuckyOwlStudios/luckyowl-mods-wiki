---
obtain: "Crafted from an Iron Chest and a Tripwire Hook (shapeless)."
summary: "An Iron Chest that gives off a redstone signal when opened, like a trapped chest."
---

Works like a vanilla trapped chest: opening it powers redstone next to it, and the more players have it open, the stronger the signal. Otherwise it's the same as the [Iron Chest](../iron_chest/). It holds as much as a chest, can be **dyed** any color by right-clicking it with a dye, and can be locked.
