The Golden Chest holds as much as a normal chest, and two placed side by side join into a double chest. It's as tough as a block of gold, and you can mine it with a pickaxe or an axe.

## Dyeing

Right-click it with **any dye** to color the wood. Use another dye to change the color again.

## Piglins

Piglins guard Golden Chests the same way they guard vanilla chests and gold blocks. Open or break one near them and they'll turn on you.

## Locking

Like any chest, it can be locked with a [Key](../key/), a [Golden Lock](../golden_lock/) or a [Trial Lock](../trial_lock/).

For a version that gives off a redstone signal when opened, see the [Golden Trapped Chest](../golden_trapped_chest/).
