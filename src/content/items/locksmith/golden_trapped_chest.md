obtain: "Crafted from a Golden Chest and a Tripwire Hook (shapeless)."
summary: "A Golden Chest that gives off a redstone signal when opened, like a trapped chest."
---

Works like a vanilla trapped chest: opening it powers redstone next to it, and the more players have it open, the stronger the signal. Otherwise it's the same as the [Golden Chest](../golden_chest/). It can be **dyed** any color, it can be locked, and piglins guard it.
