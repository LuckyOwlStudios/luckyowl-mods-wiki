Right-click a container, door or trapdoor that has a **Golden Lock** while holding a Golden Key. The lock comes off for good and the key is used up. Without one, a Golden Lock can't be opened, broken or blown up.

![A golden locked chest in a dungeon](/mods/locksmith/media/golden_locked_chest_dungeon.webp)

## Where to find them

- **Dungeon Slimes** drop exactly one Golden Key each. They live deep underground below Y 0, and in strongholds. See the [Dungeon Slime Spawn Egg](../dungeon_slime_spawn_egg/) page for where they spawn.
- **Nether Fortress chests** have a 15% chance to contain one.

About 1 in 4 Nether Fortress chests is itself golden locked, so it's worth carrying a spare key into the Nether.

Golden Keys stack to 16.
