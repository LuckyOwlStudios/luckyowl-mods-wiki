![A chest with a Trial Lock](/mods/locksmith/media/trial_locked_chest.png)

Right-click an unlocked chest, barrel, door or trapdoor with a Trial Lock to lock it. It opens with a vanilla **Trial Key**, the same key Trial Spawners drop in Trial Chambers, and the key is used up when it does. The lock itself isn't used up.

Like the [Golden Lock](../golden_lock/), it's for **map makers and server admins**. A trial locked container can't be blown up or broken by players without a Trial Key.

## Changes to Trial Keys

Locksmith also tweaks the vanilla Trial Keys:

- **Trial Keys** now say "Unlocks Vaults & Trial Locks" in their tooltip, and **Ominous Trial Keys** say "Unlocks Ominous Vaults".
- Trial Keys stack to 16.
