The Iron Chest holds as much as a normal chest, and two placed side by side join into a double chest. It's tougher, like a block of iron, and you can mine it with a pickaxe or an axe.

![Iron chests dyed red, blue and purple](/mods/locksmith/media/dye_chest.webp)

## Dyeing

Right-click it with **any dye** to color the wood. Use another dye to change the color again. Handy for color-coding a storage room.

## Locking

Like any chest, it can be locked with a [Key](../key/), a [Golden Lock](../golden_lock/) or a [Trial Lock](../trial_lock/).

![A locked Iron Chest](/mods/locksmith/media/locked_iron_chest.png)

For a version that gives off a redstone signal when opened, see the [Iron Trapped Chest](../iron_trapped_chest/).
