## The Dungeon Slime

The **Dungeon Slime** is a gray, see-through slime with a [Golden Key](../golden_key/) floating inside it. It's the main way to get Golden Keys.

- It always spawns at **medium size**, never large.
- The medium slime drops **exactly one Golden Key**. When it dies it splits into small Dungeon Slimes, which drop slimeballs instead.
- Its sounds are a little deeper than a normal slime's.

## Where it spawns

- In the **Overworld below Y 0**, in full darkness.
- In **strongholds** at any height, where about 1 in 6 hostile mobs that spawn is a Dungeon Slime.
- Never on Peaceful, and never in Mushroom Fields or the Deep Dark.

The spawn egg is for creative builders and map makers, and it works in monster spawners too.
