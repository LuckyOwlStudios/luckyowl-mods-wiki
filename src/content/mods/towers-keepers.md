**Lucky's Towers & Keepers** adds the **Keepers**, a new race for Minecraft. It runs on both **Fabric** and **NeoForge**. This mod is still being made, so details may change before release, and this page will grow as it does.

## The Keepers

Keepers take on the same jobs villagers do: Librarian, Mason, Cartographer, Fletcher, Toolsmith, Weaponsmith, Shepherd, Fisherman, Butcher, Armorer, Farmer, Cleric and Leatherworker.

Alongside them live a few special Keepers: the [Powersmith](mobs/powersmith/), who can push your enchantments past their max, the [Gildsmith](mobs/gildsmith/), and the [Tower Keeper](mobs/tower-keeper/), who stacks Keepers into living towers. See the Mobs section below for all of them.
