**Locksmith** lets you lock containers so only the right key opens them, and adds sturdier iron and golden chests that you can dye any color.

## Locking chests

- Use a **Key** on an unlocked container to lock it. The container takes on that key's **code**, and from then on only a key with the same code opens it.
- **Copy a key** by putting it in an anvil with iron, so friends can share access.
- Hold the reveal key to see a key's code in its tooltip.
- With [Curios](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/curios) installed, a key worn in a Curios slot opens matching containers without holding it.

## Unlocking chests

![A golden locked chest in a dungeon](/mods/locksmith/media/golden_locked_chest_dungeon.webp)

Some locks don't take a key code. They open for one special key, and the key is used up when the lock comes off.

- **Golden Locks** open with a **Golden Key**. Piglin Brutes drop Golden Keys, and they can turn up in Nether Fortress chests.
- **Trial Locks** open with a vanilla **Trial Key**, the same key that opens vaults in Trial Chambers.
- Put a Golden Lock or Trial Lock on any container yourself to guard loot in your own dungeons and adventure maps.
- Trying to open a locked container without the right key fails, and you're told the container is locked.

## Dyeing chests

![Iron chests dyed red, blue and purple](/mods/locksmith/media/dye_chest.webp)

**Iron Chests** and **Golden Chests** (and their trapped versions) can be dyed. Use any dye on the chest to change its color, and use a different dye to change it again. Great for color-coding your storage room.
