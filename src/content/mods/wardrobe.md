**Lucky's Wardrobe** brings more personality, style and immersion to your Minecraft world. It adds a wide variety of clothing, from cozy winter coats and scarves that protect you in cold environments (with compatibility for temperature-based mods), to aprons, hats and more. Many pieces are practical as well as good-looking, and all of them stay true to Minecraft's own visual style.

## Outfits by biome

Each biome has its own matching outfit set:

- **Snowy:** hat, hood, coat, pants and boots, built for the cold.
- **Taiga:** hood, coat, pants and boots.
- **Desert:** hat, robe, pants and sandals.
- **Jungle**, **Savanna** and **Swampy** sets, including the Savanna floral crown.

## Hats

From the simple **Hat** and **Top Hat** to the **Explorer's**, **Farmer's**, **Chef**, **Witch's** and **Blacksmith's** hats, plus animal hats: **Fox**, **Snow Fox**, **Wolf**, **Panda** and **Bee**.

## Work clothes

Aprons for every trade (leather, hardened leather, butcher's, cleric's, mason's and fishing), the **Entertainer's** outfit, the **Wool Vest**, the **Chiton** and the **Scarf**.

> A 1.21.1 remake that runs on both **Fabric** and **NeoForge** from one codebase is in progress.
