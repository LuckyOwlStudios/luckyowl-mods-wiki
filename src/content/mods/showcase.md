**Lucky's Showcase** adds neat ways of displaying your items.

- **Tool Rack:** hang weapons and tools on the wall.
- **Weapon Stand:** stand weapons and tools up for display.
- **Pedestal:** show off any item. Pedestals can be dressed with a carpet.
