**Cozyhome** adds furniture to make your builds feel lived in. It runs on both **Fabric** and **NeoForge**, and most pieces come in every vanilla wood or stone type.

## Wooden furniture

![A Cozyhome kitchen with counters, a sink and a brick chimney](/mods/cozyhome/media/chimneys.webp)

In every wood type from oak to warped:

- **Tables**, **Short Tables**, **Desks** and **Chairs**
- **Sofas** and **Couches**
- **Counters**, **Sink Counters** and **Drawers**
- **Storage Counters**, whose doors swing open while you use them
- **Wall Clocks** and **Grandfather Clocks**. The hands step forward once per in-game minute, and wall clocks can also stand on the floor, on tables and fences, or hang from the ceiling.
- **Railings** and **Large Stumps**
- **Planked Walls**, placed along any axis like logs. Three planks in a column make three walls.

## Stone & Waterworks

<img class="update-logo" src="/mods/cozyhome/media/stone_and_waterworks_logo.png" alt="Stone & Waterworks update" />

![A stone fountain in a garden](/mods/cozyhome/media/fountain.webp)

Stone furniture in andesite, granite, diorite, deepslate, tuff, calcite, sandstone, brick, mud, prismarine, purpur, nether brick, blackstone, end stone and more:

- **Bathtubs** and **Sinks**
- **Chimneys**
- **Fountains** and **Fountain Spouts**

Iron and gold versions of the bathtub, sink and chimney are included too.

![Every stone variant lined up](/mods/cozyhome/media/stone_lineup.webp)

**Any liquid works.** Sinks, sink counters, bathtubs and fountains accept any bucket, vanilla or modded, and an empty bucket takes the liquid back out. Sinks and bathtubs also draw from a fluid source placed behind them: water stays infinite, other fluids get used up.

![A lava fountain in the Nether](/mods/cozyhome/media/lava_fountain.webp)

## Themed sets

Matching chairs, tables, lamps and clocks in **Glass**, **Iron**, **Ominous** and **Undead** styles, plus the **Princess Chair**.

## Dyeing furniture

Use a dye on a **couch**, **sofa** or **lamp** to set it to exactly that dye's color, or combine one furniture piece with one dye in the crafting grid. Dyed items are named after their color, like "Red Oak Couch". There's also a **Paint Brush**, which sets a block's color, and a **Dye Vat**.

## Cushions and decor

![The Wandering Trader selling a Trader Cushion](/mods/cozyhome/media/trader_deals.webp)

- **Cushions** in all 16 colors, plus a **Hay Cushion**. Put a cushion on chairs, sofas and couches.
- The **Trader Cushion**, sold by the Wandering Trader
- **Lamps** and **Wall Mirrors**
- The **Telescope**: use it at night to check the moon's phase and learn a moon fact

## Compatibility

With [Universal Sawmill](https://modrinth.com/mod/universal-sawmill) installed, wooden counters, storage counters, sink counters, clocks, wall mirrors, lamps, sofas and couches can be made at the sawmill from planks or logs. It's optional.
