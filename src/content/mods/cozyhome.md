**Cozyhome** adds furniture blocks to make your builds feel lived in. Every piece comes in all vanilla wood types, from oak to warped.

- **Tables** and **Short Tables**
- **Counters**, **Storage Counters** and **Sink Counters**
- **Railings** and **Planked Walls**
- **Cushions** in all 16 colors, plus a **Hay Cushion** and a **Trader Cushion**
