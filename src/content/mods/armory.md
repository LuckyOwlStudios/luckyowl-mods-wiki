**Lucky's Armory** adds a wide variety of light and heavy armor variants crafted from **iron**, **gold**, **diamond** and **netherite**. Customize your combat style with new gear that is **dyeable** and **trimmable**, with visual toggles such as opening a heavy helmet's visor.

## Light armor

A full light set (helmet, chestplate, leggings and boots) for each of iron, gold, diamond and netherite.

## Heavy armor

A full heavy set for each material. Heavy helmets can be shown opened or closed.
