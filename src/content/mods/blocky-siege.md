**Blocky Siege** adds placeable siege weapons to Minecraft. Load them, aim them and bring down your enemies' walls (or your friends').

- **Cannon:** fires powerful cannon rounds. Open it to aim and set the firing power.
- **Burst-Cannon:** a four-round burst cannon, with a settable burst count.
- **Spewer:** shoots liquid and spreads it over a large area.
- **Volley Rack:** shoots a volley of projectiles at once.
- **Ballista**

There is a **Fire Cannon** key binding for firing the weapon you are manning.

## Ammunition

**Cannonballs**, **Bullets**, **Explosive Kegs** and **Explosive Barrels**, plus elemental bombs: the **Fire Bomb**, **Frost Bomb** and **Wind Bomb**, each with its own impact.

## Villagers and banners

The mod adds a new villager profession, the **Siege Engineer**, and the **Fort** and **Boom** banner patterns.
