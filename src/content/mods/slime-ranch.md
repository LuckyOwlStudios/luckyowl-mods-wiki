**Lucky's Slime Ranch** adds new kinds of slimes, a way to grow them, and bouncy blocks made from their slime. It runs on both **Fabric** and **NeoForge**. This mod is still being made, so details may change before release.

## Slimes

Every new slime has a spawn egg.

### Cave Slime

A gray slime with no face. It has 1.5 times the health of a normal slime and comes in three sizes. It spawns in Overworld caves below Y 50, in the dark, but not in Mushroom Fields or the Deep Dark.

### Bouncy Slime

A blue slime that hops 1.7 times as high as a normal one, about 3.5 blocks. It takes no fall damage and hits with strong knockback. It drops **Bouncy Slime Balls** and spawns wherever normal slimes do.

### Ice Cube

An icy light blue slime. Each hit freezes you a little more, like powder snow, and about two hits freeze you solid. Leather armor protects you. It spawns in snowy biomes, in the dark, in groups of one or two, and drops snowballs as well as slime balls.

### Pinky

A small, harmless pink slime with 30 health that hops every few ticks. It spawns in Cherry Groves on grass in daylight, in groups of two to four, and drops one to three slime balls.

## Mooshroom Milk

Use an empty bucket on an adult mooshroom to fill it with **Mooshroom Milk**. Use the milk on any slime, magma cubes included, to grow it to the next size (1, then 2, then 4). You get the bucket back, and a slime you've fed will no longer despawn. Pinky can't be grown.

## Bouncy blocks

- **Bouncy Slime Block:** made from 9 Bouncy Slime Balls. It bounces you 1.25 times as hard as a slime block, and it isn't sticky.
- **Bouncy Piston:** a piston crafted with a Bouncy Slime Ball. Instead of pushing blocks, it launches them as falling blocks, and it launches mobs and players too.
